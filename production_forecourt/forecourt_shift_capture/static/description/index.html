Forecourt Shift Capture & Reconciliation

Extends forecourt_operations to capture the 14 daily shift data points hand-recorded by shift managers and runs four reconciliation checks in the background.

  • Opening stock vs previous shift’s system closing stock.
  • Opening + received − sold vs closing stock.
  • ATG closing vs DIP closing (per fuel grade).
  • Σ(cash-sales litres × fuel rate) vs (cash on hand − POS account).

Adds a Shift Capture tab on the shift form, a forecourt.shift.fuel.entry child model, global per-shift cash / POS / lube / LPG fields, and a date-effective forecourt.fuel.rate table (seed: Petrol K27.15, Diesel K33.99).
